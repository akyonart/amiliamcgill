La Compagnia

Case Study

  • Brand Identity

La Compagnia is a contemporary Italian long-table dining concept where every meal is a shared experience. Inspired by commedia dell'arte, it celebrates the drama, personality, and spontaneity of human connection. Each gathering is unique — filled with small stories, playful improvisation, and the warmth that comes from being fully present with others. The restaurant encourages everyone to bring their own character to the table, creating memorable experiences that are as lively, imperfect, and joyful as the people around it.

Moodboarding

The visual direction draws deeply from the improvisational spirit of Commedia dell'arte and Italian street performance. To capture this distinct charm without feeling dated, I layered the tactile warmth of old tarot card illustrations against the precise, eccentric symmetry of a Wes Anderson set. The result is a mood board that defines a 'theatrical' dining experience—one that feels like a staged performance where the diners are the cast.

The first iteration

Drawing from the mood board's focus on old card drawings, the first iteration explored a 'deck of cards' aesthetic. I designed the menu layout to mimic an uncut sheet of playing cards, utilizing a grid system to showcase the different character archetypes. This theme extended to the wine packaging, where I translated rough marker sketches into clean, geometric vector illustrations that retained a hand-drawn, whimsical quality.

In Good Company

The name "La Compagnia" is a play on words — inspired by the company you share at the table and the company that brings life to the night.

An Evening in Acts

Each course is an act, unfolding alongside the personalities gathered at the table. Inspired by the lively Italian stock characters, the menu moves with curiosity, conversation, and spontaneity. Every guest brings their own story, every interaction adds flavor, and together the evening becomes a unique shared experience.
