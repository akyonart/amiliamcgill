ONSI is a magazine that captures the energy, creativity, and camaraderie of the young, urban climbing scene. Its visual language bursts with vibrant colours, bold geometric shapes, and textured details, reflecting both the sport’s dynamic physicality and the diversity of its community. Through playful design and an inviting tone, ONSI creates a space where readers feel connected, inspired, and encouraged to embrace both the challenges and the joys of climbing culture. Project Role & Execution: As Art Director and Lead Designer, I developed the full editorial design system from concept to print-ready files. Using Adobe InDesign for layouts, Illustrator for custom graphics, and Photoshop for image preparation, I crafted a cohesive identity that balances bold, energetic visuals with clear, readable storytelling. From typography choices to photo art direction, every detail was designed to make the magazine feel vibrant, approachable, and uniquely reflective of the climbing community.

Why ONSI.

ONSI, short for “onsight,” a climbing term for tackling a route blind. Even if you’ve never climbed, ONSI captures the energy of community, encouragement, and curiosity: trying, failing, learning, and cheering each other on.
