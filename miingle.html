Miingle

  • Product Development

Neurodivergent individuals often face barriers to casual social interaction in conventional spaces, which can feel rigid or overstimulating. Miingle is a pop-up café experience designed to break those barriers, offering an adaptable, inclusive environment that encourages connection at every pace. The fluid, evolving brand reflects the space itself, making socializing approachable, comfortable, and memorable for everyone.

Where Connections Flow

With fluid branding that shifts and adapts, the logo itself mirrors the essence of Miingle: growth, adaptation, and authentic human connection.

Blooming in space

Deconstructing the sunflower symbol for autism, the petal-tables offer a flexible landscape for interaction. The design celebrates growth not as a straight line, but as a blossoming series of new connections.
