Overview

Wild Bird Trust is a bird sanctuary that aims to protect and preserve local bird species through education, conservation, and community engagement.

Challenge

How can a rebranding project for a bird sanctuary effectively communicate its mission and values to the community while maintaining a strong visual identity?

Solution

Through a thoughtful rebranding process, the Wild Bird Trust project successfully communicated its mission and values to the community by creating a strong visual identity that reflects the sanctuary's commitment to conservation and education. The new branding incorporated natural elements and a clean, approachable aesthetic that resonated with both existing and potential supporters.
