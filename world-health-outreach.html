Overview

World Health Outreach is a global non-profit focused on community-led health and education. The project translates the organization’s grassroots mission into a professional identity that balances the reality of field work with the long-term vision of empowerment.

Challenge

How can a visual identity represent the realities of frontline health work while communicating hope, dignity, and community empowerment?

Solution

The identity centers on a dual-purpose mark that visualizes the mantra "Walking Together, Rising Together." The logo mimics a boot print to symbolize the hard miles walked in partnership with communities, while also forming a rising sun to represent the transformative power of health and education.
